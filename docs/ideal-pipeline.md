## Simplified Pipeline (What Matters)

### 1. **Intelligence Gathering** ⚡
- Crawl target site (15+ pages, depth 3)
- Crawl competitors (top 3-5)
- Fetch Moz keyword data (volume, difficulty, suggestions)
- Extract all existing content, structure, metadata

### 2. **Keyword Strategy** 🎯
- Generate keyword opportunities (Moz + competitors + site)
- Filter irrelevant garbage (navigation, cookie consent, etc.)
- AI sense-check (does "sunday roast" apply to a nightclub?)
- Prioritize by: volume × relevance ÷ difficulty
- **Output Section:** "Here's what people are searching for that you should own"

### 3. **Site Architecture** 🏗️
- Analyze what pages competitors have
- Identify gaps (they have /services/custom-tattoos, you don't)
- Map keywords to pages (which page targets which keyword cluster)
- Design pillar + cluster structure
- **Output Section:** "Here are the 12 pages your site MUST have to compete"
